Our story

Momentum began with one camera, a borrowed car and a wedding in Connemara {{img landscape-06|Green cliffs|120px}} in 2013. Thirteen years later we're a team of six — still obsessed with the in-between moments everyone else misses.

We started Momentum because we were tired of photographs that looked like everyone else's. Too posed, too filtered, too in a hurry. We wanted to make pictures that felt like memory — the laugh just before the kiss, the father who can't look at his daughter yet, the light that only lasts a minute.

Today we photograph around 40 weddings a year — deliberately few — alongside portraits, editorial and brand work for people and companies we genuinely admire. Every gallery is still edited by hand, by the photographer who shot it. That will never change.

What we believe

Three things we won't compromise on.

01

Presence over poses.

We direct when it helps and disappear when it doesn't. The real moments are always better than the ones we could invent.

02

Craft in every frame.

No presets, no outsourcing. Each image is graded by hand by the person who took it, so your gallery feels like one story.

03

Fewer, better clients.

We cap our calendar so every couple and brand gets our full attention — before, during and long after the shoot.

The team

The people behind the lens.

Small by design. Every one of us shoots, edits and genuinely cares how your story is told.
